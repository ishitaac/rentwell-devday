Juniper Row Apartments

SECTION 1. PREMISES AND TERM

1.1 Premises. Unit 3B, a 2 bedroom / 1 bath apartment at 1450 Fictional Avenue, Salt Lake City, Utah ("Premises").

1.2 Term. This Lease begins January 1, 2027 and ends December 31, 2027 ("Term").

1.3 Occupancy. The Premises may be occupied only by the persons named in this Lease. Maximum occupancy is two (2) persons. A guest may stay no more than 14 days in any 6-month period without Landlord's written consent.

SECTION 2. MOVE-IN COSTS

2.1 Security Deposit. Resident will pay a security deposit of $1,000.00 at signing. Landlord may deduct from the deposit a carpet cleaning charge of $150.00 at move-out regardless of the carpet's condition, plus any unpaid amounts and any damage beyond normal wear and tear.

2.2 Administrative Fee. Resident will pay a non-refundable administrative fee of $250.00 at signing.

2.3 Application Fee. A non-refundable application fee of $45.00 per adult applicant was paid before this Lease was signed.

2.4 Access Devices. Resident will receive two (2) key fobs for a deposit of $25.00 each, refundable when the fobs are returned in working condition. A lost or damaged fob will be replaced for $60.00 each.

2.5 First Month. The first month's rent and all monthly charges are due at signing.

SECTION 3. RENT

3.1 Base Rent. Base rent is $2,100.00 per month, due on the first (1st) day of each month. Rent will remain fixed for the Term.

3.2 Rent Schedule. The rent schedule in Exhibit A is part of this Lease.

3.3 Late Charges. If rent is not received by the end of the third (3rd) day of the month, Resident will pay a late fee equal to five percent (5%) of monthly base rent, plus $15.00 for each additional day the rent remains unpaid.

3.4 Returned Payments. A fee of $40.00 applies to any returned or declined payment.

3.5 Payment Method. Payments by credit or debit card carry a convenience fee of 2.9% of the payment. Payment by ACH or check carries no fee.

SECTION 4. MONTHLY CHARGES IN ADDITION TO RENT

4.1 Reserved Parking. Resident must lease one (1) reserved parking space at $100.00 per month. A second space, if available, is $75.00 per month. Guest parking is limited. Unauthorized vehicles may be towed at the vehicle owner's expense.

4.2 Community Amenity Fee. Resident will pay $45.00 per month for use of the fitness room, clubhouse, and courtyard, whether or not Resident uses them.

4.3 Technology Package. Resident will pay $65.00 per month for the community internet service, which is required for all units.

4.4 Pest Control. Resident will pay $8.00 per month for pest control services.

4.5 Valet Trash. Resident will pay $25.00 per month for doorstep trash collection.

SECTION 5. PETS

5.1 No pets are allowed without a signed Pet Addendum. If a pet is approved, pet rent is $35.00 per month per pet and a refundable pet deposit of $300.00 per pet applies.

SECTION 6. COMMUNITY RULES

6.1 Resident agrees to follow the Community Rules, as amended by Landlord from time to time. The Community Rules are available at the leasing office. Violations may result in fines set by Landlord.

SECTION 7. MAINTENANCE AND ENTRY

7.1 Landlord will make repairs to the Premises in a reasonable time after written notice. Resident will pay for repairs needed because of damage caused by Resident or Resident's guests.

7.2 Landlord may enter the Premises with at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice, or at any time in an emergency.

8.1 Resident may not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord may withhold in its discretion. If Landlord consents, a transfer fee of $150.00 applies.

SECTION 9. LIABILITY OF RESIDENTS

9.1 If more than one person signs this Lease, each is jointly and severally liable for the full rent and all other obligations under this Lease. If one Resident moves out, the remaining Resident(s) remain responsible for the entire amount.

SECTION 10. EARLY TERMINATION

10.1 Resident may end this Lease before the end of the Term by giving at least sixty (60) days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' base rent. Resident remains responsible for rent and charges through the end of the notice period.

10.2 The early termination fee is in addition to any unpaid charges and deposit deductions.

SECTION 11. RENEWAL AND HOLDOVER

11.1 At the end of the Term, this Lease converts to a month-to-month tenancy unless Resident gives written notice of intent to vacate at least sixty (60) days before the end of the Term. Month-to-month base rent is the then-current base rent plus $250.00 per month.

SECTION 12. GENERAL

12.1 This Lease is the entire agreement between Landlord and Resident. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties, except as stated in Section 4.8.
